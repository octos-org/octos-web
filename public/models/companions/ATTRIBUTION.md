# Learning companion model sources

The Panda, Penguin, and Bee models are selected from Quaternius's **Cute
Animated Monsters Pack** (August 2020).

- Creator: Quaternius
- Source: https://quaternius.com/packs/cutemonsters.html
- Download mirror: https://opengameart.org/content/textured-cute-monster-pack
- License: CC0 1.0 Universal
  (https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/)

The original animated FBX meshes and PNG textures were converted and embedded
as browser-ready GLB files for Octos. The character artwork remains available
under CC0; attribution is not required, but is included here to preserve clear
asset provenance.
